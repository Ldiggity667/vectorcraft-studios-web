Frequently Asked Questions

Planning information for quote requests.

How does quoting work?

Send a request with your files or a description; we review scope and confirm availability, price, materials, and timing in a written quote before any work starts.

What files can I send?

STL, OBJ, STEP, 3MF, PDF, DXF, PNG, or JPG — attach the file to your quote email (up to 10 MB).

What materials do you print in?

PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, Nylon, TPU, and Carbon Fiber PETG; fit and availability are confirmed in your quote.

Can you print in a material you don't list?

Yes. Send a special material request with the material type and what it's for. We confirm availability, the material cost, and any processing it needs — such as annealing or special equipment — in your written quote.

What can be engraved?

Names, dates, and messages; logos and artwork; serial numbers and identifiers — working area 380 × 370 mm.

Are the calculator numbers final prices?

No — estimates only, excluding tax and shipping; the written quote is the price.
